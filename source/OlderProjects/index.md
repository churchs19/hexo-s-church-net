These are projects I built and supported in the past. They're no longer maintained, and the Windows Phone Store they were sold in has been shut down, but the pages are kept here for reference.

## Windows Phone apps

### [Stirling Money](/StirlingMoney/)
A checkbook register replacement with budgets, financial goals, charts, OneDrive backup, and family sync. Machine translated into 37 languages. A cross-platform [Flutter rewrite](https://github.com/churchs19/stirling-money-flutter) is in progress.

### [Stirling Birthday](/StirlingBirthday/)
Shows your friends' upcoming birthdays on a live tile, with quick call, text, or email greetings. Localized into 38 languages.

### [What I Eat](/WhatIEat/)
A simple food journal that lets you choose how detailed your entries are, with color-coded meal types and export to Excel through OneDrive.
